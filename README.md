# UMBC Counseling Center Scheduling System

A systems analysis and design project proposing a redesigned appointment scheduling system for the UMBC Counseling Center, developed as a graduate team project in the M.S. Information Systems program.

## Problem

Student demand for campus mental health services rose sharply during and after the COVID-19 pandemic, while the Counseling Center's intake and scheduling process remained largely manual. This created long wait times, scheduling bottlenecks, and limited visibility into capacity — at exactly the moment students most needed timely access to care.

## Approach

The project followed a full requirements-to-design lifecycle:

- **Requirements gathering** — interviewed Counseling Center staff and surveyed the student body to document current-state workflows and pain points
- **Contextual research** — analyzed the 2023 UMBC operating budget and regional reporting on college mental health demand to ground the proposal in real institutional constraints
- **System modeling** — produced context models, use case diagrams, UML action diagrams, and sequence diagrams covering the core flows (account creation, appointment scheduling)
- **Final proposal** — consolidated findings, models, and recommendations into a written report and stakeholder presentation
